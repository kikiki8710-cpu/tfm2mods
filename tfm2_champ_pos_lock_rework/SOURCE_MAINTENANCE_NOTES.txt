[Important Rework invariants]
- Safety requirement is dynamic; never hard-code 20 as a universal value.
- A position below the safety target still keeps its designated position mask.
- Designated champions are used first.
- Unassigned champions are free-reserve and stay locked until a designated pool is actually exhausted.
- Exhaustion opens reserve fallback only for the affected position; champions designated to other positions do not become free.
- Shared-pool/subset safety is evaluated only across actually overlapping designated positions.
- Direct player / AI / head coach / AI assignment / auto-swap must share the same rules.
- Global fail-open is the final anti-deadlock emergency only.
